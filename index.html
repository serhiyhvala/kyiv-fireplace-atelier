How a build runs

From a measured wall to a first fire

Four steps, and you know the price and the date before any brick is ordered.

  1. 01

    1–2 hours

    Visit

    I come to your home, measure the wall, check the chimney and the floor load, and ask how you plan to use the fire.

  2. 02

    Within a week

    Drawing

    You get a scaled drawing and a fixed quote. You choose the brick, stone or tile, and we change the drawing until it is right.

  3. 03

    4–8 weeks

    Build

    I build on site, course by course. Drying time between stages is part of the schedule, so nothing is rushed or cracked.

  4. 04

    Half a day

    First fire

    We light the first fire together. I show you how to burn dry wood, what to sweep and when, and how to care for the masonry.

Questions

Before you book

Not answered here? Call +380 44 555 01 23 or write to studio@example.com.

How long does a build take?

An open hearth takes 4–6 weeks, a tiled stove 6–8 weeks. Masonry needs time to dry between stages, and I put that time in the schedule you receive with the drawing.

Can I have a wood fire in a flat?

Only with a chimney of its own, a floor that carries the weight, and the building’s approval. I check all three on the visit, so you know before you commit to a build.

Do you work outside Kyiv?

Yes, within about 100 km of the city. Further away is possible by arrangement, with travel added to the quote.

Can you work from an architect’s drawings?

Yes. Send the plans before the visit and I will check the firebox, chimney and clearances against them, then draw the build to match.

How do payments work?

Half when you approve the drawing, to order materials, and the rest at the first fire. The visit fee is deducted from the first payment.

What wood should I burn, and how often do I sweep?

Dry hardwood such as oak, beech or hornbeam, split and stored for at least a year. Sweep the chimney once a year before the season starts.
